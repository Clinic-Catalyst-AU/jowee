The pathway

Educate. Assess. Recommend.

Hypnosis stops being a separate thing to sell. It becomes part of how every new body contouring client starts with you.

01

Educate

Short videos, posts and Highlights that explain why the body side sometimes stalls when the mind isn't on board: the cravings, the self-sabotage, the "I know what to do but I don't do it". Plain words, no jargon.

02

Assess

A short suitability assessment on your website, landing pages and Instagram bio. It asks about her goals, what she has tried and what gets in her way, and captures her details.

03

Recommend

Her answers point her to the path that suits her, and her follow-up messages match that path until she books her consultation.

For every new body contouring client: "Are you open to an assessment for suitability?"
