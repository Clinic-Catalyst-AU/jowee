The pathway

Educate. Assess. Recommend.

Hypnotherapy stops being a separate thing to sell. It becomes part of how every new body contouring client starts with you, and the system does the sorting so you never have to have the awkward conversation.

01

Educate

An education area, landing pages and posts that answer the common questions and fears, including the big one: "If I put the weight back on, will I have to do this all again?"

02

Assess

Every new client coming in for fat freezing or body contouring goes through a short eligibility assessment, with a few pointed questions that show whether hypnotherapy would suit her.

03

Recommend

If she's eligible, she's asked straight up whether she's open to it. It's your duty of care to show her every tool that will get her the result she told you she wants.

"You're eligible to include hypnotherapy as part of your treatment plan. Is that something you're open to?"
If she says no

Straight to booking

"Awesome, let's get you booked for your treatment." She's booked for her body contouring, simple and warm.

If she says yes

Your hypnotherapy stream

She still books her body treatment, and she joins a list of clients who are pre-qualified and ready, with a short video series dripped to her while she waits.

Your capacity, your call

Open the doors when you're ready

You don't book everyone into hypnotherapy at once. When you open capacity, the list gets a hot offer, and they're already warm.
